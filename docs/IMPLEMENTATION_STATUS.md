# Implementation status and full-scope ledger

Audit date: 2026-11-06 UTC. This ledger records the actual repository implementation, not completion of the product goal. The [PRD](<../Urban Planning — Product Requirements.md>) and accepted implementation plan remain the target; missing capabilities have not been removed from scope.

**Status meanings:** **Implemented** means the named local behavior exists with an identifiable implementation/check. **Partial** means only part of the requirement works. **Pending** means the requested capability is not implemented. **External prerequisite** identifies data rights, credentials, independent evidence, or infrastructure not supplied by code alone. A row may have more than one status.

## PRD §3 — Place, activity, population, and authoring

It permits three new requests per account per UTC day, ten total attempts per UTC day, one active worker, and at least 60 seconds between attempts. Production preparation is disabled unless explicitly enabled by the operator. These limits preserve the full product objective while larger-area ingestion remains unfinished; see [API preparation details](../apps/api/README.md#national-place-search-and-bounded-preparation).

Building drafts hold full geometry and the development program together until one Apply command. Components plus common floor area must reconcile to declared GFA; top-level homes/jobs/use mirror the components. Trip and parking overrides retain their denominator and source. Existing captured legacy programs keep their earlier interpretation until explicit adoption; source geometry is never silently simplified or rewritten during program-only changes. Converted type defaults are previewed before replacing floors, height, program quantities, or parking supply. Overlapping building footprints and parcel straddlers produce warnings; shared parcel coverage unions footprints once, while each building program is counted once per feature. Removing a building leaves parcel boundaries in place; absent mapped structures are not proof of vacant legal land.

Extended buildings use project format 3, including captured baselines and past/undone commands; the version never downgrades after undo. Unchanged legacy format 1/2 records retain their optional-field absence and source hashes. New authored geometry allows 500 points per ring and 5,000 vertices per feature. Programs allow up to 64 components. Full geometry, component assumptions, provenance, and typed variants survive local/server saves, frozen public snapshots, project/GIS exports, and bounded PDF/Word/workbook inventories. Model version 0.4.0 handles explicit program calculations. Zoning ordinance packs, automatic setbacks/variance explanations, locally calibrated rates, and floor-by-floor or different wing heights remain outstanding.

Street drafts are separate from applied scenario changes: users add or move points in 2D, enter exact coordinates, then apply or discard. Changed paths are capped at 500 points and validated along every segment against the exact study area, including holes and separate components; unchanged captured paths remain eligible for settings-only edits. Optional nearby-vertex snapping is geometric alignment, not junction creation. New streets use labeled user-proposal provenance and estimated two-lane, 25 mph defaults with no AADT. Reshaping in the editor clears a retained AADT and records `geometrySourceId` separately from the original source and attribute provenance. Applied additions, reshapes, and removals update road/lane/bike-lane lengths, saved project history, publication geometry, and GIS/report exports while preserving the baseline. Older routing evidence is excluded after a revision changes; no new traffic counts, congestion, or accessibility results are invented.

Parcel/block drafts retain every ring and land component until a valid preview is applied. Same-kind features may touch but cannot overlap area; block/parcel overlap is allowed. Limits are 500 points per open ring plus closure, 5,000 total vertices per feature, 5,000 scenario features, and 50,000 overlap candidate pairs; imports additionally allow 50,000 total vertices. Invalid, outside, no-cut, sliver, and excessive-complexity results fail explicitly. Straight-line subdivision preserves original land and holes; assembly retains disconnected pieces as a MultiPolygon. No operation changes the captured baseline or creates a surveyed lot by implication.

Applied parcel operations and reviewed imports create one reversible history step. Full geometry, source references, `sourceFeatureId`, and split/assembly lineage persist through saves, publications, and exports. Parcel-bearing projects use format 2 and cannot be downgraded; untouched legacy format 1 and baseline hashes remain unchanged. Current and historical states are validated, including undo/redo targets. The inspector's baseline comparison uses the same selected proposal boundary; aggregate scorecard comparisons use each side's own parcel set. Whole-building associations never assign straddling floor area by centroid or proportion, while coverage uses the exact clipped footprint union. These distinctions preserve the remaining authoritative-data, ordinance, and professional-validation requirements.
